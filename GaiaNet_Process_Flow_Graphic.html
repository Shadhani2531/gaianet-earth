GaiaNet Digital Twin System Process

Acquisition of Real-time Data
Ingesting heterogeneous API payloads from NASA FIRMS, WAQI, and Open-Meteo.
Multi-modal Data Fusion
Standardizing spatial topologies and temporally caching the environmental variables.
Incorporating 4D Environment
Integrating the synchronized data overlay onto the CesiumJS Liquid Glass HUD.
Identifying Climate Anomalies
Using 6-month historical sliding windows to isolate extreme temperature variables.
Causal Cascade Prediction
Calculating associative exponential probabilities (Drought → Wildfire escalations).
Sustainability Health Indexing
Synthesizing AQI and anomaly thresholds into human-readable Grades (A-D).
Simulating Intervention Scenarios
Running "what-if" models (+1.5C / +2.0C warming) across global geographical sectors.
Citizen Science Ground Truthing
Overlaying spatial crowdsourced incident reports on authoritative macro-data.
Actionable Climate Interventions
Informing proactive, policy-driven disaster response and resiliency pathways.
