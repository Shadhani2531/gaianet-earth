The Global Sustainability Health Index is calculated from four independent data sources: air quality (OpenAQ), carbon emissions per capita (World Bank), life expectancy (WHO), and vegetation cover (NASA MODIS). Each country's score reflects its standing relative to the full range of values observed across all sampled countries. Country coverage varies by data source; scores are weighted proportionally across the components for which real data is available.
